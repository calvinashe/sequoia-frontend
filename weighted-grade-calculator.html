How to calculate a weighted grade

Each category (exams, homework, labs…) is worth a set slice of your grade. Your overall grade is the weighted average: multiply your score in each category by its weight, add them up, and divide by the total weight.

overall = Σ ( score × weight ) ÷ Σ ( weight )

Example

Exams 40% at 85%, Homework 30% at 95%, Labs 30% at 90%.

= (85×40 + 95×30 + 90×30) ÷ 100 = 89.5% overall.
