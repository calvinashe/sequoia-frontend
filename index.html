Connect your Canvas

Takes about 60 seconds. Your token stays on your device — we never store passwords.

Token encrypted · Never shared · Read-only access
1
Open Canvas → click Account (your photo, top-left)
2
Click Settings
3
Scroll down to Approved Integrations → click + New Access Token
4
Name it "Sequoia", set the expiry date to , then click Generate Token and copy it
⚠ Copy it now — Canvas only shows it once.

Connect your Calendar Feed

Paste your school calendar feed URL — works with Canvas, Blackboard, Moodle, D2L, and any LMS that exports .ics. Syncs your assignment names and due dates, no token needed.

How to find it: Canvas: Calendar → 📅 icon (bottom right) → copy feed URL Blackboard: Calendar → Settings gear → Export → copy .ics link Moodle: Calendar → Export → copy the generated URL
